The 24 JPEGs under images/ are the `sd` (2048 x 1024) derivatives of street-level pictures published on
Panoramax (https://panoramax.fr) by the producer `sig_bayonne` and hosted on panoramax.ign.fr. Each picture's
STAC item (panoramax-items.json.gz, `properties.license`) declares the licence `etalab-2.0`:
Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0, https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/licence-ouverte-open-licence/

Attribution: sig_bayonne via Panoramax, Licence Ouverte / Etalab 2.0.

They are committed unmodified, as served on 2026-10-06, as the measurement input of
reports/2026-10-06-non-gsv-levelling.md (#190). sample.json records each file's sha256 and size, and
reports/scripts/non_gsv_levelling.py refuses to analyse a file that does not match.

The remaining files here are API responses: panoramax-items.json.gz (Panoramax STAC items) and
adminapi-panos-<city>.json.gz (Project Sidewalk's public /adminapi/panos for each city).
